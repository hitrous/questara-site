Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

What this app is

"questara-factory" is a private tool run by the owner of the Questara YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own videos and edits their titles and descriptions through the YouTube Data API.

Data we access

The app uses a Google authorization of the channel owner's own account, limited to YouTube upload and channel management. It does not access the data of any other person, and it has no public users or sign-in.

Data we store

We do not collect, sell, share or store personal data of visitors or viewers. The authorization token is kept as an encrypted secret in the owner's private repository and is used only to upload videos to the Questara channel.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
